# Zonr technical design — pilot

Code baseline: `main` `99d283e` (VERIFIED). Pilot behavior: `FRD.md`. This file is the record shape. Decisions D-067 through D-102 fix the rules they name. S-1 and S-3 are accepted (D-092). S-2 is D-067. S-4, S-5, and S-6 stay UNAPPROVED (`PRD.md` section 4). It is not a deployed service. Henry personally owns the IP. The notice is [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md) (D-096). It is not an open-source license.

Labels: VERIFIED, INFERRED, UNKNOWN, PROPOSED. Statute text is UNKNOWN and is not designed here. Route it to RADAR.

## 1. Purpose

Define the records, the states, the transitions, the invariants, the failures, and the permissions for the pilot, including the tenancy principle that is in the schema and not in the product surface.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In the pilot design

`Community`, `CommunityConfig`, `Account`, `Session`, `Invite`, `MagicLink`, `GuestPass`, `Vehicle`, `Violation`, `ViolationPhoto`, `TowAuthorization`, `AuditEvent`, `AccessLog`, `ScanEvent`, `DeletionLog`, `Notification`, `AdminAlert`, `GuestLink`. Files for photos and for the signed writing, keyed by `community_id`, private.

### 2.2 Not in the pilot build

A second community's UI, payments, cameras, property-system sync, numbered-spot geometry, deletion or tokenization of a community, and any client of a tow vendor (approved scope, plus `PRD.md` section 8). The mock's module store (`src/services/mockApi.ts`) is the prototype only (VERIFIED). The pilot replaces that seam. It does not extend `tow-dispatched`.

## 3. Success

The schema can hold the pilot community and a second id without a migration of column meanings (PROPOSED, UNAPPROVED, S-6). Permission checks match the PAC ids in `FRD.md`.

## 4. Non-goals

| Non-goal | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Choose a cloud vendor | Not required to freeze the records. Login is an email magic link (D-097), not a vendor choice |
| Treat RADAR's §22658 list as a legal conclusion | D-104 adopts it as PROPOSED. An attorney confirms it before the pilot goes live |
| Store 75 stall rows | Spots are unnumbered. The count sits on the community profile (D-094) |
| A delete screen for plates, photos, or phones | Purge is a job under the D-103 schedule, not an API a resident calls |

On the requests PAC-104 names, authority fields are not taken from the client. Guest-link read, invite acceptance, and the operator bootstrap are the exceptions in I-2.

`month_cap_subject` for the pilot is household (D-081). `month_window` is a calendar month. `timezone` is the IANA name `America/Los_Angeles`, stored as data. A missing row still refuses the create. Code does not assume that zone when the row is missing. The mock's permit `unit` and guest `hostUnit` are strings (VERIFIED, `src/types.ts`). They are not `household_id`.

The month total counts stored passes whose interval overlaps that Pacific calendar month, in any of `scheduled`, `active`, derived `expired`, or `revoked` (D-081). The daily total counts passes whose `created_at` falls on the current calendar day in that timezone, including a pass later revoked (D-100). The daily number is `per_day_cap` and is unset (Q-034). If the household id is missing on `for_account`, the create is refused. The cap subject is `for_account`. When the issuer is an admin, that is the named resident, not the admin, unless the admin account is itself that resident. A later edit of the account does not change ids already stored on a pass (PAC-075). When only the month cap fails, the error is "This household has used its 4 guest passes for this month." When only the daily cap fails, the error is "This household has reached today's guest-pass limit." When both would fail, no row is stored and the sentence is Q-035. A duration over 72 hours returns "This pass is longer than 72 hours." even if a household cap would also fail (D-080).

The plate recorded on a vehicle at time t is the `after` plate on the latest audit event for that vehicle whose action is vehicle create or a plate or description edit, and whose `at` is less than or equal to t. When two such events share `at`, the later appended event is the one that counts. Hold and remove events record status in `before` and `after`. They are not plate events. The vehicle row keeps the current plate and description. While the audit event is still kept, the export can also show the previous and new plate (PAC-023, PAC-060).

The plate that was warned is the `plate` stored on the violation at filing. That field does not point at the vehicle row, and it does not depend on the audit event still existing after 365 days (D-074, D-101). A warning for a plate that no vehicle carried at that time still keeps the submitted plate. Signing copies that same violation plate onto the authorization (PAC-051). That copied plate is on the authorization row. It also remains after the audit event is purged. During the 365 days, the audit history can still be compared with the violation plate. After the audit purge, the violation plate and the authorization plate are the record of what was filed and what was signed. An open violation keeps its plate. The age purge does not delete it. A closed violation's plate is deleted only when that row is past 36 calendar months from `closed_at` and its photo files are already gone (PAC-065). The plate on a signed or voided authorization is deleted with that row 36 calendar months after `signed_at`. An unsigned draft has no signed plate. It is not deleted while the violation is open, and it is deleted 36 calendar months after that violation's `closed_at` (Q-049). The violation clock is a PROPOSED reading of D-101 (Q-048). A warning-only or fined photo can already be gone under its stored `purge_after` (D-103). After that photo purge the violation row still shows its id, plate, reasons, times, filers, grace end, and close reason, and it shows no image (Q-043).

## 6. States and transitions

Clocks in this section are the server clock (PAC-103). No transition below is a tow dispatch.

### 6.1 Guest pass

| From | To | Who or what | Guard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | `scheduled` | Resident or admin (D-082) | `start > now`. Caps against `for_account`, config, and PAC-006 |
| — | `active` | Resident or admin (D-082) | `start <= now < end`. Same guards |
| `scheduled` or `active` | `expired` | Read-time comparison | `now >= end`. Not a background job. Not permission (PAC-005). The guest link stays viewable for 24 hours after `end` if the pass is not revoked (D-088) |
| `scheduled` or `active` | `revoked` | Issuing resident, or an admin for any pass in the community (D-082) | Session actor recorded (PAC-007). The guest link becomes the invalid line immediately |

A read treats a stored `scheduled` or `active` row as permission only when `start <= now < end`. When a `scheduled` row enters that window, the read treats it as `active` without a writer. No transition creates a violation.

D-067 (Henry, 2026-10-23, VERIFIED as his statement): a resident adds, updates, and removes their own vehicle with no admin pre-approval. An admin may remove any registered vehicle in the community or put it on hold. There is no approval status and no admin add of a vehicle for another account. A session without resident is refused on add. The mock still lets a resident save a status of approved (VERIFIED, `PRD.md` section 7). The pilot does not copy that write. `community_id` is required (I-1). D-077 (Henry, VERIFIED as his statement): a plate check of an `on_hold` vehicle returns status `on_hold` and action `none`, and the line is "on hold, don't act". A warning and a tow authorization for that plate are refused while the vehicle stays `on_hold`. There is no dispatch endpoint. Release returns the usual warning and tow rules (PAC-034). D-069 (Henry, VERIFIED as his statement; `PRD.md` section 11.1): a resident cannot edit or remove a vehicle that is `on_hold`, including when that session also has admin. The request is refused and the row stays `on_hold`. Only an admin removing the hold frees that vehicle for resident edits and removal. D-075 (Henry, VERIFIED as his statement): only an admin can release a hold. A resident and a patroller cannot. The edge is `on_hold` to `active`. That target is derived from D-069, which frees the vehicle for resident edits, and those edits exist only while the status is `active`. Henry can correct the target status. Admin removal of an `on_hold` vehicle still sets `removed` (PAC-025) and is a different action from removing the hold. Nothing restores a `removed` row. D-073 (Henry, VERIFIED as his statement; `PRD.md` section 11.1): 3 plate changes per vehicle per 30 days. Enforcement is the refusal. The 1st, 2nd, and 3rd plate changes in the window are stored. The 4th is refused, the vehicle row is unchanged, and no plate-change audit event is appended. A plate change is a successful edit of that vehicle id whose normalized plate in `after` differs from `before`. Create is not a plate change. A description-only edit is not a plate change. Two vehicles of one resident each have their own count. A later vehicle that reuses a plate after `removed` starts at zero. The window is D-099: rolling, on the server `at` of those plate-change audit events, counting an event while `now - at < 30 × 24 hours`. The event drops out of the count when `now - at >= 30 × 24 hours`, including at exactly 30 × 24 hours. The community timezone is not applied. At that boundary another plate change is allowed if fewer than 3 events still count. The audit row stays until the 365-day purge. Admins already see every plate change: the same audit event stores `before` and `after` plate, the actor, the vehicle id, and `at` (PAC-062). No second log is added. Each refused 4th writes an `AdminAlert` (D-100). The daily guest-pass cap is the other D-100 control, and its number is Q-034. The 30-day count uses only events whose age is under 30 × 24 hours. D-074 purges an audit event only once it is past 365 days, so that job does not remove an event the count still uses, and it does not remove an event that has dropped out at exactly 30 × 24 hours. D-072 (Henry, VERIFIED as his statement): an admin hold and an admin removal each write a `Notification` for that vehicle's resident. A resident removal does not. D-098 sends that notice by email and by text. The wording is Q-037. D-102 uses the same send for a release.

### 6.3 Violation

| From | To | Who or what | Guard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | `warning` | Patroller or admin | No other unclosed warning for that plate. At least one photo with step `first`. Grace configured as 3 × 24 hours. `warning_reason` is a code from PAC-047. `warning_reason_text` is non-empty only for `other`. An empty or unknown code, `other` with empty text, and text on another code are refused and no row is stored. Grace has ended when the stored status is `warning` and `now >= grace_ends_at`. That is a read-time comparison. It is not a stored `grace_elapsed` state and not a job. It does not by itself unlock PAC-050. The second warning must also exist (D-085). It does not hide the warning on plate check (PAC-030, PAC-044).

### 6.4 Tow authorization

| From | To | Who | Guard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | `draft` | Signer | Same community, warning not closed, `now >= grace_ends_at`, and `second_warning_at` set. Storing the draft sets `had_tow_authorization` true on the violation in the same transaction. A refusal stores no row and does not change the flag (PAC-050). If the second warning is missing, the error is "A second warning is required before a tow authorization." | `draft` | `signed` | Signer | Artifact stored under PAC-051. No outbound call (PAC-052). The same `on_hold` refusal as the draft (PAC-102, D-077) |

A void does not delete the warning. Closing the violation does not sign a draft (PAC-102). The unsigned draft then stays until PAC-065, which deletes it 36 calendar months after `closed_at` and does not clear `had_tow_authorization`. A draft on a still-open violation stays.

## 7. Invariants

| Id | Invariant | Label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I-1 | Every row's `community_id` references a community | PROPOSED |

## 8. Privacy and retention

Adopted pending California attorney review (D-103). Not a legal conclusion. Do not read a statute out of this table. D-074 is unchanged: the audit log is 365 days. D-101 keeps a violation record and a tow authorization for 36 months and does not name the clock. The PROPOSED reading (Q-048) is 36 calendar months from `closed_at` for a closed violation, and 36 calendar months from `signed_at` for a signed or voided tow authorization. An unsigned draft has no `signed_at`. It stays while the violation is open, and it is deleted 36 calendar months after that violation's `closed_at` (Q-049). An open violation is not deleted by that job. The plate on the violation is the plate-at-warning proof. It is not the vehicle plate.

In one run the photo job runs first. It deletes a file whose `purge_after` is past and does not consult the violation or the tow authorization. The unsigned-draft purge runs next and deletes a draft whose violation is past 36 calendar months from `closed_at`. A signed or voided row is then deleted when it is past 36 calendar months from `signed_at`. The violation-row job runs last. It deletes a closed row that is past 36 calendar months from `closed_at`, has no photo file left, and has no tow-authorization row left, including no draft. It skips an open violation. It skips a closed row that still has a photo file. It skips a closed row that still has a draft, so the violation purge does not leave an orphan draft. A legal hold on the draft keeps that draft and therefore keeps the violation row. The tow-authorization purge, including the draft purge, does not clear or change `had_tow_authorization`. The violation row still carries that flag after the authorization is gone. Close copies the flag onto the photos. It does not look for an authorization row.

A day period is kept while `now - at` is less than or equal to N × 24 hours, including exactly that age, and purged when `now - at` is greater. A month period uses calendar months on the server clock, not Pacific time. A record at exactly that many calendar months is kept. A record past that date is purged. There is no delete screen. Each purge, redaction, or blocked purge writes a `DeletionLog` row.

`legal_hold` on a row is the legal-hold flag. The column is on Account, Invite, GuestPass, Vehicle, Violation, ViolationPhoto, TowAuthorization, AuditEvent, ScanEvent, and AccessLog. An admin sets it when the row is tied to a dispute, a claim, a subpoena, or a member records request, and clears it when that matter is resolved. While the flag is set, no purge and no redaction touches that row. The job writes `held` on the deletion log. After the flag is cleared, the next run applies the age rule, including a row that is already past its period.

## 12. Questions

Decisions are in `PRD.md` section 11.1. D-069 resolves Q-021: a resident cannot edit or remove an `on_hold` vehicle, and only an admin removing the hold frees it for those actions. D-070 resolves Q-022: a resident may register any number of vehicles, and add does not count that account's vehicles. D-073 resolves Q-023: 3 plate changes per vehicle per 30 days, refused on the 4th, and admins see those changes on the existing audit events. D-072 resolves Q-024: an admin hold and an admin removal each write a `Notification`. A resident removal does not. D-074 resolves Q-025: an audit event is kept for 365 days and deleted once it is past that age. The plate stored on a violation, and the plate copied onto a tow authorization, stay after that deletion. D-075 resolves Q-026: only an admin can release a hold, and the status becomes `active`. That target is derived from D-069. Henry can correct it. D-076 resolves Q-027: an empty warning reason is refused. `warning_reason` is a required code, and `warning_reason_text` is required only for `other`. The codes and labels are PROPOSED in `PRD.md` section 11.2 and in `FRD.md`. That list is the only PROPOSED part of D-076. D-077 resolves Q-020: a plate check of an `on_hold` vehicle returns status `on_hold` and action `none`, and the line is "on hold, don't act". A warning and a tow authorization for that plate are refused until an admin releases the hold.
